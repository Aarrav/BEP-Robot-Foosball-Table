## Senden et al. 2022
- **Citation:** Senden et al., IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) 7(3), 2022. Full citation: TODO
- **What it did:** Overhead RGB camera, self-calibrating world model, 100 Hz vision loop, 1 kHz motion loop. Reactive strategy: the nearest man aligns with the ball and kicks when in reach. Tested on stationary ball positions only.
- **What it lacks / relevance:** No latency or ball-speed figures, no prediction, no velocity in the world model; motion control not discussed.
- **Status:** read
